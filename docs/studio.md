# The studio

`apps/studio/` is the project's settings, a client beside the window on the same kit, laid out as a canvas: its sections are a carousel down the left edge, the one on screen at the top beside the bar of its commands, folded to their numbers until the pointer or the keyboard is in them, or a tap on the head, unfolds them over the canvas, the bar ranking its commands as the kit's `PageBar` does — BACK apart, the main action solid (an ADD, EDIT, SAVE), the others outlined, DELETE and DISCARD in the error tone — 1 to 8 (or Ctrl with them) and Ctrl+Tab turning it. The first section, the flow, draws the project as a flowchart over the whole window: you, then each depth a row, where each agent a work may go to on its own stands alone at the head of the row, where the chart is framed from, then each workflow a work there may go through is a chain of its stages, even where the depth requires one, and the agents only workflows take close it, a line from everything that hands works down (an agent with `work` or `delegate`, or a workflow one of whose stages has it) to every place below; each workspace a zone with its own lists, and each agent or workflow a pack brings marked with the pack's name. Below its actual size the chart is drawn at that size and reduced through mipmaps, so it stays smooth. It frames itself again when the window changes size until it is moved by hand, never below the smallest size its cards read at. Dragging moves it, a wheel zooms around the pointer, a touchpad moves it and zooms with Ctrl, two fingers pinch it, and the zoom's indicator in the footer zooms out and in, brings it to its actual size and frames it all, as - + 0 and F do; Tab reaches each agent's box, and Enter or a click picks it, Esc lets it go; picking an agent shows it beside the canvas, from where it opens in the agents section; with several projects in the registry, the corner names each and picking one draws it. The flow's bar holds what the project can be given — an agent, a workflow, a model, a provider, a workspace — each opening the section it is added in; when the names do not fit, each drops its ADD. Coming to a section decodes its names and notes again. The other sections — secrets, models, agents, workflows, tools, skills and the project (its policy, its access and its workspaces) — are each a table of their items, headed by the section's name, the packs' agents and workflows among them: a click picks a row and the bar offers to add, view or delete; a double click or Enter opens the item on a screen of its kind (`ProviderView`, `ModelView`, `AgentView`, `WorkflowView`, `ToolView`, `SkillView`, `PolicyView`, `AccessView`, `WorkspaceView`, each an `ItemPage` over an `ItemDraft`), read as three columns the height of the window, each scrolling by itself: its image (an agent's, fetched once by its SHA-256 into Quickshell's cache through `Http.fetch`) with its facts under it, its description, and what is related to it (its model, provider, workflows, skills, tools, the agents on it, ...), each related item opening in its section, whose BACK comes back to the screen it was followed from; stacked, and the page scrolling, on a narrow window; its bar goes back, edits (Ctrl+E), deletes and, editing, saves (Ctrl+S) through `configure`, discards or cancels. The policy's screen edits `assemble`, `max_retries`, `max_reopens` — left empty, a parent reopens a work as often as it likes — `last_run_output_bytes` and `tool_output_bytes`, the default workspace and workflow, and the ceiling of every run. What a pack brings, the tools and the skills are only read; a skill's screen gives it to the project's agents. The `Studio` singleton reads the setup through the `config` tool, the catalog through `tools`, the passwords through `omapass` and, for every project `GET /v1/projects` lists, its `overview` on that project's own socket, all through `/v1/cli`, and tries a model through `model_test`. `apps/kit/run studio` takes the server registry and a project id, which may be left out when the registry holds one project.
